# Turnout video script

For the video "Turnout demo - talk over this" (2:39). Read each part when the video reaches its time.

---

**0:00** · the empty form

Brands pay for reach they can't check, and community organisers pay for events out of their own pocket. Turnout lets a brand fund real community events and pay only for the people who showed up.

---

**0:18** · typing the request

A brand tells Commfy what it wants: here, a new oat drink wants feedback from active people in East London, with a budget of fifteen hundred pounds.

---

**0:41** · Commfy's recommendation, the slider, then PayPal

Commfy's AI picks three communities and says why. It also recommends how the organisers get paid: this brand wants feedback, so most of the pay rewards survey answers. The brand can adjust it, then approves in PayPal. PayPal holds the whole budget, and each organiser gets 30% base pay straight away.

---

**1:21** · the door code and the phone

At the door, members scan a code that changes every 30 seconds, on their own phone. Answering two quick questions puts one pound from the brand into their wallet, with the brand's name on it. No app, no account.

---

**1:51** · the crowd, the bonus, paying out

After the events, each organiser is paid on results: people there, survey answers and new faces, each up to its cap. The brand awards a bonus to the community that shared the night. PayPal captures only what was earned; the rest never leaves the brand.

---

**2:18** · the proof report and the cash-out

The brand gets a proof report: who came, who answered, new faces, how welcome they felt and their answers, with no personal data. Members cash out to PayPal.

---

**2:30** · back to the top

Turnout. Brands pay only for people who showed up. Commfy: find your people IRL.
